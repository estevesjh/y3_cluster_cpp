\section{The shared $P(k,z)$ and its consistency}
\label{sec:pk}

All cross-code comparisons consume one CAMB $P(k,z)$, generated once at
the fiducial cosmology with $\sigma_8$ matched by $A_s$ rescaling
(achieved $\sigma_8 = \sigmaEightAchieved$), on the dump's own grids
(50 redshifts in $[0,4]$; 506 wavenumbers in
$[10^{-4}, 50]\,h/$Mpc) so the pinned test benchmarks remain
comparable. The production pipeline's $P(k)$ comes from the
\modname{cp\_camb} emulator: against real CAMB it agrees to
\pkDumpDevMax{} (max) / \pkDumpDevMedian{} (median) over the full grid
--- emulator error is negligible for every comparison in this report.
