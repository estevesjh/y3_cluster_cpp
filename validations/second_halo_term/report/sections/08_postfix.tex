\section{Post-fix results and downstream re-validation}
\label{sec:postfix}

With the fix in place, the production tables are finite everywhere,
genuinely $z$-dependent, and land on the references:
$\Shh$ at $z = 0.408$ matches the CLensPy benchmark to $0.8\%$
(pinned at 3\%), the default-method $\DShh$ matches the CLensPy
$\Delta\Sigma$ benchmark to $1.0\%$ (pinned at 3\%), and the published
\blk{Wp\_hh} reproduces the per-$z$ $\ximm$ pins to $2.4\%$ (pinned at
4\%; the residual is log-interpolation off the 50-point $\xi$ grid at
the smallest pinned radius, identical at every $z$ because linear
growth cancels in the ratio).
