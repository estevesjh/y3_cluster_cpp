\section{Pre-fix baseline}
\label{sec:baseline}

Driving the unfixed \modname{ct\_2hTerm} through the exact production
call site on the shared $P(k,z)$ reproduces every pinned defect
bit-for-bit: all 50 redshift rows of $\Shh$ and \blk{Wp\_hh} identical;
\nanFracBefore{} of $\DShh$ non-finite with the NaN edge at
$R = \nanEdgeR$ cMpc$/h$; the published (z-invariant) $\Shh$
overshooting the true $z = 0.41$ two-halo term by
\sigmaDevBeforeMax{} at $R = 3$ falling to \sigmaDevBeforeMin{} at
$R = 20$ (Table below), and \blk{Wp\_hh} missing the true
$\ximm(r, z{=}0.41)$ by up to \wpDevBefore.
