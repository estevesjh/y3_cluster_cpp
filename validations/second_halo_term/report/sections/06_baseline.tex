\section{Pre-fix baseline}
\label{sec:baseline}

Driving the current (unfixed) \modname{ct\_2hTerm} through the exact
production call site on the shared $P(k,z)$ reproduces every pinned
defect bit-for-bit: all 50 redshift rows of $\Shh$ (and of the chain's
internal $\xi$) identical; \nanFracBefore{} of $\DShh$ non-finite with
the NaN edge at $R = \nanEdgeR$ cMpc$/h$; the published $\Shh$
overshooting the true $z = 0.41$ two-halo term by \sigmaDevBeforeMax{}
at $R = 3$ falling to \sigmaDevBeforeMin{} at $R = 20$ (Table below);
and the internal $\xi$ missing the true $\ximm(r, z{=}0.41)$ by up to
\wpDevBefore. The
harness additionally decomposes the $\Shh$ deviation: it is a
\emph{composite} of the z-degeneracy (the $z{=}0$ row is $\sim$53\%
above $z = 0.41$) and the dummy-NFW contamination injected by the
view-mutation bug (an additive $\Sigma_{\rm NFW}(10^{14}, c{=}5)$
profile, dominant at small $R$) --- e.g.\ at $R = 3$:
$2.15 + 1.65 = 3.80\,\Msunpc$, exactly the issue-\#4 table value.
