\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

The DES cluster-cosmology pipeline
(\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.06593}{DES Cluster et al.\ 2023})
publishes a conventional two-halo lensing table ---
\blk{haloModel/Sigma\_hh} and \blk{haloModel/dSigma\_hh} --- produced
by
\modname{ct\_2hTerm} in \pyfile{y3\_buzzard/haloModel.py} and consumed
by the traditional $1h{+}2h$ ``max-model'' composition
(\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.00039}{McClintock et al.\ 2019}).
(The chain's historical third output, \blk{Wp\_hh} --- its internal
$\xi$ under a misleading $W_p$ name --- has been dropped from the
datablock entirely during this exercise: unsupported, nothing in the
reference pipeline reads it, and its only legacy reader interpolated
it on the wrong axis.)
GitHub issue \#4 recorded three confirmed defects:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{z-degenerate tables} --- the per-redshift loop passed the
  full two-dimensional $P(k,z)$ array to cluster\_toolkit's
  \texttt{xi\_mm\_at\_r}, whose C interface silently consumed the first
  row only: every redshift slice of $\Shh$ (and of the chain's
  internal $\xi$) was $P(k, z{=}0)$.
\item \textbf{NaN clipping} --- every negative $\DShh$ value was
  overwritten with NaN, blanking \nanFracBefore{} of the table (all
  $R \lesssim \nanEdgeR\,$cMpc$/h$).
\item \textbf{Broken stabilizer} --- the analytic-NFW add/subtract pair
  (a \emph{numerical} device, not exclusion physics;
  Section~\ref{sec:fix}) used mass $M_d$ on the add/subtract side but
  $M_d/10$ inside \texttt{DeltaSigma\_at\_R}, breaking its exact
  cancellation and producing the negatives that defect 2 masked.
\end{enumerate}

This report documents a staged validation of the whole transform chain
\[ P(k) \;\to\; \xi(r) \;\to\; \Sigma(R) \;\to\; \Delta\Sigma(R), \]
each stage checked two independent ways: (i) against
\emph{closed-form} density profiles whose full lensing chain is
analytic (NFW, Einasto), and (ii) against independent codes
(cluster\_toolkit, CLensPy, pyccl, clmm) fed one common CAMB
$P(k,z)$ at the fiducial cosmology. A failure therefore localizes to a
single transform. Section~\ref{sec:xinl} extends the audit to the
$\xinl$ table that feeds the selection-bias ($\bsel$) and
shear-projection branches --- the priority item identified during this
work. Everything numerical in this document is generated by the
harness in \pyfile{validations/second\_halo\_term/} (never
hand-edited); \texttt{run\_all.sh} reproduces it end to end.
