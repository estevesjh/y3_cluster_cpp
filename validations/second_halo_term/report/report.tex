\begin{abstract}
The two-halo lensing tables \blk{haloModel/Sigma\_hh},
\blk{dSigma\_hh}, \blk{Wp\_hh} produced by
\modname{ct\_2hTerm} carried three confirmed defects: a z-degenerate
table (every redshift row was $P(k, z{=}0)$), a NaN clip blanking
\nanFracBefore{} of $\DShh$, and an inconsistent dummy-halo mass that
broke the NFW-sandwich stabilizer. We validate the full transform chain
$P(k)\to\xi(r)\to\Sigma(R)\to\Delta\Sigma(R)$ in stages --- against
closed-form NFW and Einasto profiles, and against cluster\_toolkit,
CLensPy, pyccl and clmm at a common CAMB $P(k,z)$ --- fix all defects,
and select the $\Delta\Sigma$ method by measurement
(direct interior-mean integration: \dDirect{} vs sandwich \dSand{}
against a converged anchor). A dedicated section audits the
$\xinl$ table feeding the $\bsel$ and shear-projection branches: the
table and its consumers are numerically correct, but every run to date
has fed it \emph{linear} $P(k)$ (halofit input does not exist yet);
at $z=0.41$ halofit/linear reaches \xiNlLinRatioROne{} in $\xi(r{=}1)$
and \dsNlLinRatioRThree{} in $\DStwo(R{=}3)$.
\end{abstract}
